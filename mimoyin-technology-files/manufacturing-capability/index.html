Manufacturing Capability

Mimoyin is a Shenzhen-based manufacturer specializing in ePTFE waterproof breathable membranes, waterproof acoustic vent membranes, and pressure equalization vent solutions for consumer electronics, wearables, automotive electronics, and industrial devices. Since 2018 we have focused on one thing: helping device makers solve waterproofing, acoustic transparency, and pressure-balance challenges with reliable membrane materials and precision converting.

Quality Control & Testing

Every order is verified before shipment. In-house testing covers the parameters that matter to electronics customers:

  • Air permeability / air-tightness — verified with a dedicated air-tightness tester
  • Water entry pressure — measured with a water pressure tester to confirm IP67/IP68-rated protection
  • Dimensional accuracy — caliper inspection against customer drawings
  • Visual & appearance — high-resolution camera inspection of each part

Material Compliance

Our ePTFE membrane materials are RoHS 2.0 compliant. Testing was performed by Centre Testing International (CTI) — a CNAS/CMA-accredited laboratory — under IEC 62321 methods.

Test report No.A2240129904101001E
SampleePTFE waterproof and breathable film (white)
Tested forPb, Cd, Hg, Cr(VI), PBBs, PBDEs, Phthalates (DBP, BBP, DEHP, DIBP)
ResultAll 10 restricted substances — Not Detected (N.D.)
DateMarch 2024

This means the membrane we supply is safe for use in electronics exported to the EU and other RoHS-regulated markets.

Engineering Support

An acoustic vent membrane is a thin porous film that blocks liquid water while letting sound pass — the core component that protects a device’s speaker or microphone from ingress. Mimoyin supplies this as a converted component matched to your housing.

Most projects start with a drawing and a protection requirement. Our engineer supports you through:

  • Membrane material selection (ePTFE / TPU / PU) based on your waterproof, acoustic, and pressure needs
  • Prototype samples matched to your specifications
  • Acoustic and waterproof performance tuning
  • Reliability validation support (IP rating, aging, temperature cycling)

We work on a custom-per-spec basis — tell us the application and the constraint, and we propose the membrane and converting solution.
