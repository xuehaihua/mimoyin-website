Wearable Waterproof Breathable Membrane

Our wearable waterproof breathable membrane is designed for smart watches, fitness trackers and other wearable electronic devices. It provides reliable protection against sweat, water and dust while maintaining stable performance of internal sensors and acoustic components.

Why Wearable Devices Need Vent Membranes

Wearable electronics are constantly exposed to sweat, rain and humidity. Without proper protection, moisture can damage internal circuits, microphones and pressure sensors. Our ePTFE membrane solves this by balancing air pressure while blocking liquids and contaminants.

Core Advantages

  1. IP67/IP68 waterproof and sweatproof protection for daily sports use
  2. Stable air permeability for pressure balance in compact devices
  3. Prevents fogging and condensation inside watch housing
  4. Ultra-thin structure suitable for mini wearable design
  5. Custom die-cut shapes for small electronic enclosures

Main Applications

  • Smart watches
  • Fitness trackers and smart bands
  • Sports wearable devices
  • Medical monitoring wearables
  • Wireless health tracking devices

Material Options

  • ePTFE waterproof breathable membrane
  • TPU laminated breathable film
  • PU soft breathable layer for comfort protection

Challenges in Wearable Device Design

  • Continuous sweat and skin-oil exposure during sports and daily wear
  • Water ingress from rain, hand washing, and swimming
  • Pressure imbalance inside sealed, compact housings
  • Condensation that degrades mic preamp and pressure-sensor readings

Recommended Membrane Materials for Wearables

TPU Membrane (Mainstream Choice)

  • High flexibility for curved and wraparound wearable forms
  • Soft, lightweight structure that improves wearer comfort
  • Cost-effective for high-volume consumer production

ePTFE Membrane (Premium Solution)

  • Superior durability and chemical/environmental resistance
  • Stable waterproof performance under harsh outdoor use
  • Acoustic-transparent grade keeps mic sound clear

TPU vs ePTFE for Wearable Applications

  • TPU: Flexible, lightweight, cost-effective — ideal for smartwatches and fitness bands
  • ePTFE: High durability, low acoustic loss — preferred for premium and rugged outdoor wearables

TPU dominates consumer wearables, while ePTFE is chosen for high-end or outdoor-rugged devices where longevity matters most.
