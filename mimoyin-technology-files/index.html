Technical FAQ

Common questions from B2B buyers about Mimoyin waterproof membrane solutions

What waterproof breathable membranes does Mimoyin manufacture?

ePTFE, TPU, and PU microporous membranes for IP67/IP68 protection, custom die-cut for automotive, TWS audio, and wearable electronics.

What industries do your membranes serve?

Automotive electronics, TWS earbuds, smart wearables, consumer electronics, medical devices, microphones and speakers, and industrial sensors.

Can you match a membrane to my specification?

Yes. Send your drawing with required breathability, acoustic loss, and water pressure; we select the material and produce die-cut samples for validation.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Prototyping is supported with free samples (3–5 day delivery); mass-production MOQ is flexible per specification.

What waterproof ratings are achieved?

IP67 and IP68, with ePTFE grades resisting equivalent water pressure up to 50 m.
