What Is a Waterproof Acoustic Membrane?

A waterproof acoustic membrane (often made of ePTFE, TPU, or PU) is a specialized microporous film designed to protect sensitive electronic components like TWS earbuds, microphones, and speakers from water, dust, and debris, while allowing sound waves and air pressure to pass through with minimal loss. Mimoyin’s advanced waterproof acoustic membranes provide superior acoustic performance and reliable environmental protection for your audio devices.

ePTFE vs. TPU/PU Acoustic Membranes

While TPU and PU membranes are cheaper, they often sacrifice acoustic performance and long-term durability. ePTFE offers:

  • Higher porosity → better airflow & sound transmission
  • Superior chemical & thermal resistance
  • Longer service life (5–10 years vs. 2–3 years)
  • Better hydrophobicity (water beads off easily)

Technical FAQ: Waterproof Acoustic Membranes

What is the sound transmission loss of a waterproof acoustic membrane?

Our ePTFE acoustic membranes keep sound loss ≤1 dB (measured against Japanese reference films), far below PU/non-fluorine films (6–10 dB). Lower loss preserves voice clarity at the microphone.

How does the membrane block water but let sound through?

The microporous ePTFE film has pores smaller than water droplets yet larger than air/sound wavelengths, so sound passes while liquid water is repelled — achieving IP67/IP68 at the mic port.

What IP rating can a microphone waterproof membrane reach?

IP67 and IP68. We match the membrane's water-pressure rating to your device's required depth during sampling.

Can the acoustic membrane be die-cut for tiny microphone ports?

Yes. We laser- or punch-die-cut to round, rectangular, or custom shapes down to small port sizes, in a Class 10,000 cleanroom.

Which applications use waterproof acoustic membranes?

TWS earbuds, Bluetooth speakers, smartphones, hearing aids, and outdoor IoT microphones that need voice clarity plus water/dust protection.
