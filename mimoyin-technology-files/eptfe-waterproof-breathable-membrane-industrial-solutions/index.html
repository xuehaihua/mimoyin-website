Industrial Application Solutions of ePTFE Waterproof Breathable Membrane

Traditional sealing solutions only block moisture without balancing internal cavity pressure. Under long-term temperature fluctuation, rain and dust, failures such as shell bulging, internal condensation and component short circuit frequently occur. With micro-pore structure, ePTFE waterproof breathable membrane realizes integrated protection of waterproof, dustproof and pressure relief to solve sealing pain points of all kinds of industrial precision equipment.

Made of expanded PTFE substrate with 0.2~0.5μm pores smaller than water droplets but larger than air molecules, it stably reaches IP66~IP68 high protection rating. It resists temperature from -40℃ to 130℃, acid, alkali, salt spray and UV aging with stable air permeability for long service life.

Solution 1: New Energy Lithium Battery Industry

Power battery packs and energy storage power supplies release tiny gas during charging and discharging, accumulated pressure inside sealed housings easily causes shell deformation and adhesive separation. Custom self-adhesive die-cut vent membranes from Mimoyin continuously exhaust internal gas to balance pressure, eliminate battery bulging, condensation and water ingress, no extra pressure relief valves required to simplify product structure.

Solution 2: Automotive Electronics Supporting

Vehicle sensors, radars, electronic control units and charging pile boxes work in mud, water and oil. Day-night temperature difference easily generates condensate and corrodes circuit boards. Automotive-grade ePTFE vent membranes pass salt spray and temperature cycling tests, stick firmly with temperature-resistant adhesive, quickly drain internal moisture to guarantee long-term stable operation of auto electronics.

Solution 3: Outdoor Industrial Control & Security Equipment

Surveillance cameras, PLC control boxes and outdoor LED lamps face huge day-night temperature difference. Alternating cold and heat creates negative pressure to suck moisture, leading to lens fogging and circuit short circuit. The vent membrane blocks external rain and dust while balancing cavity pressure to fundamentally eliminate condensation and reduce equipment failure rate.

Solution 4: Consumer Electronics & Smart Wearables

TWS earphones, smart watches and action cameras are frequently exposed to sweat and rain. The ultra-thin composite vent film as thin as 0.15mm does not occupy narrow shell space, balancing IP67 waterproof performance and pressure balance to prevent button jamming, muffled sound and water damage.
