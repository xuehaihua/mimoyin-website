Introduction

Waterproof breathable membranes are essential in electronics, automotive systems, and industrial protection. The two most widely used materials are ePTFE and TPU membranes.

ePTFE Membrane TPU Membrane

This guide compares their structure, performance, and applications to help you select the right solution for your project.

What is ePTFE Membrane?

ePTFE (expanded Polytetrafluoroethylene) is a high-performance microporous membrane known for exceptional chemical resistance, durability, and superior waterproof-breathable properties. It is the preferred choice for harsh-environment applications such as automotive electronics, industrial sensors, and outdoor equipment.

What is TPU Membrane?

TPU (Thermoplastic Polyurethane) membrane is a flexible polymer film that combines waterproof protection with excellent elasticity and acoustic permeability. It is widely adopted in consumer electronics, including TWS earbuds, smartwatches, and wearable devices.

Which One Should You Choose?

Choose ePTFE membrane if your application requires maximum durability, chemical resistance, and stable performance in extreme temperatures or aggressive environments.

Choose TPU membrane if your design prioritizes flexibility, acoustic performance, and cost efficiency—especially for consumer electronics and wearable devices.
