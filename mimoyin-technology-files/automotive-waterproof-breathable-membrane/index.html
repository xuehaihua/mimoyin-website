Automotive Electronics Waterproof Breathable Membrane

Automotive Waterproof Breathable Membrane

Our automotive waterproof breathable membrane is designed for vehicle electronic systems that require reliable sealing, pressure equalization and long-term environmental protection. It prevents water, dust, oil mist and condensation from damaging sensitive automotive components.

Why Automotive Electronics Need Vent Membranes

Modern vehicles contain many sealed electronic units. During temperature changes, pressure differences and humidity build-up can cause condensation, deformation or failure. Our ePTFE membrane solves these problems by allowing air exchange while blocking liquids and contaminants.

Core Advantages

  1. Stable performance from -40°C to +260°C automotive environments
  2. IP67/IP68 waterproof and dustproof protection for vehicle systems
  3. Prevents condensation inside sealed electronic housings
  4. Resistant to oil mist, fuel vapor and chemical exposure
  5. Custom die-cut shapes for automotive module integration

Main Applications

  • Engine control units (ECU)
  • Automotive sensors (pressure, temperature, ADAS systems)
  • New energy vehicle battery packs
  • On-board charging systems
  • Car lighting and control modules
  • Vehicle communication and infotainment systems
