What Is a Waterproof Acoustic Membrane for Speaker?

A waterproof acoustic membrane (also called an acoustic vent or sound-permeable membrane) is a thin microporous film placed over the speaker sound hole. Its job is threefold: block liquid and particles, let the speaker equalize pressure and breathe, and pass sound with minimal loss. In Bluetooth speakers, smart speakers, walkie-talkies, and outdoor audio devices, this membrane is the difference between a product that survives a rain test and one that fails in the field.

How Does ePTFE Acoustic Membrane Work?

Expanded PTFE (ePTFE) is stretched into a node-and-fibril microporous structure. Three properties matter for speakers:

  • Microporous structure — pores are small enough to stop water droplets by surface tension, yet large enough for air and sound waves to pass.
  • Hydrophobic ePTFE — the surface repels water, so the membrane resists wet-out even under splash or immersion.
  • Air permeability & acoustic transparency — gas flows through the pores for pressure equalization, while the film's low acoustic resistance keeps sound loss below 1 dB.

The result is a vent that "breathes but does not leak" — exactly what a sealed speaker enclosure needs.

How to Select a Waterproof Acoustic Membrane Supplier

When you shortlist an ePTFE membrane supplier, check these seven points:

  1. Material capability — do they control ePTFE expansion, or just trade film?
  2. Acoustic testing — can they show insertion-loss data, not just a claim?
  3. Waterproof testing — IP67/IP68 validation reports per design.
  4. Airflow measurement method — insist on the orifice size and pressure used.
  5. Die-cut tolerance — hold ±0.05 mm on critical shapes.
  6. Mass production — clean-room converting, lot traceability.
  7. Sampling lead time — how fast can they return a prototype from your drawing?

FAQ

What is the best waterproof membrane for speakers?

ePTFE acoustic membrane is the widely used choice because it keeps the speaker waterproof while preserving sound. It typically shows ≤1 dB loss versus 6–10 dB for TPU or non-fluorine film.

Can waterproof acoustic membranes reach IP68?

Yes. Customized ePTFE acoustic vents can meet IP67 or IP68 depending on membrane grade, adhesive, and housing design. Validation should be done on the assembled device, not the film alone.

Do you provide OEM acoustic membrane solutions?

Yes. Custom thickness, shape, adhesive, and die-cut are available per customer drawing. We prototype from your spec rather than selling fixed SKUs.

Will the membrane muffle the speaker?

No. Low acoustic resistance ePTFE passes sound with minimal attenuation; well-designed vents stay under 1 dB loss.

What airflow number should I specify?

Specify airflow with its test condition. Mimoyin measures at a Ø10 mm orifice, 7 kPa differential, reporting 200–12,000 ml/min depending on grade.
