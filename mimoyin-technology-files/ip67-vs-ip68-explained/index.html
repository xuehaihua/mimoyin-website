IP67 vs IP68: What's the Difference and Which Do You Need?

IP67 vs IP68: What's the Difference and Which Do You Need?

A practical guide to ingress protection ratings for waterproof electronics and membrane selection.

What Do IP67 and IP68 Mean?

IP (Ingress Protection) ratings are defined by IEC standard 60529. The first digit (6) indicates complete protection against dust. The second digit indicates water resistance:

  • IP67 — Dust tight + immersion up to 1 meter for 30 minutes
  • IP68 — Dust tight + continuous immersion beyond 1 meter (depth and duration specified by manufacturer)

Side-by-Side Comparison

How to Choose the Right Rating

Choose IP67 When:

  • The device may be splashed, rained on, or briefly submerged
  • Cost is a primary concern
  • Typical applications: TWS earbuds, fitness trackers, handheld tools

Choose IP68 When:

  • The device operates in permanently wet or submerged environments
  • Pressure equalization is critical (battery packs, automotive sealed units)
  • Safety standards demand continuous waterproofing (EV, industrial)
