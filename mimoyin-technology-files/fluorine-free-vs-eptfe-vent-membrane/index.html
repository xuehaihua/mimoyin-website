Difference Between Fluorine-Free Waterproof Breathable Membrane & ePTFE Membrane

We compare material, air permeability, temperature resistance, corrosion resistance, environmental protection, service life and cost to help customers select suitable waterproof vent membrane.

1. What are the raw materials of fluorine-free membrane and ePTFE membrane?

Fluorine-free breathable membrane mainly uses PU, PET, PP polymer materials. ePTFE membrane is made of expanded polytetrafluoroethylene.

2. Air permeability gap between fluorine-free membrane and ePTFE membrane under same size

With same thickness and aperture, ePTFE air permeability is 3~8 times higher than fluorine-free PU membrane, with faster pressure relief and more stable air balance.

3. High & low temperature resistance and salt spray corrosion comparison

ePTFE works stably from -40℃ to 260℃, excellent in acid, alkali and salt spray resistance.
Fluorine-free membrane shrinks at high temperature and cracks at low temperature. Its service life is only 1/3~1/2 of ePTFE membrane.

4. EU export environmental standard differences

ePTFE meets RoHS, REACH and PFAS compliant standards, suitable for global export.
Fluorine-free membrane has no PFAS restriction, but worse long-term environmental stability.

5. Why fluorine-free membrane is not recommended for lithium battery & energy storage equipment

Fluorine-free membrane melts easily under thermal runaway, causing delayed pressure relief and safety hazards. ePTFE is non-flammable, high temperature resistant and explosion-proof.

6. Can fluorine-free membrane meet IP67 waterproof demand for household small appliances?

It can reach IP67 in normal indoor environment, but poor temperature resistance and aging resistance.

7. Anti-UV aging performance under long outdoor sunlight exposure

Fluorine-free membrane clogs and fails in 3~12 months outdoors.
ePTFE maintains stable air permeability for 3~5 years under outdoor UV exposure.

8. Purchasing cost and comprehensive after-sales cost comparison

Fluorine-free material cost is 30%~60% lower. But high failure and after-sales cost in harsh conditions make total cost higher than ePTFE.

9. Can fluorine-free PU membrane & ePTFE membrane be laminated with adhesive & non-woven fabric and die-cut?

Both support adhesive lamination, non-woven composite and precision die-cut processing.

10. Which membrane to choose for tracker, sports camera and industrial terminals?

Outdoor precision electronics, trackers, sports cameras choose ePTFE waterproof membrane.
Low-cost indoor household products can choose cost-effective fluorine-free membrane.
