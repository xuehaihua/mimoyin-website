Acoustic Waterproof Membrane

Mimoyin Technology Co., Ltd. manufactures high-performance TPU acoustic waterproof membranes designed for TWS earbuds, wearable audio devices, and smart electronics. Our membranes achieve ≤1dB acoustic loss while maintaining IP67 waterproof protection.

How TPU Acoustic Waterproof Membrane Works

TPU (Thermoplastic Polyurethane) acoustic membranes use a dense, semi-porous structure that allows sound waves to pass through with minimal attenuation (≤1dB loss) while blocking liquid water. The material's inherent flexibility and hydrophobicity make it ideal for TWS earbuds, smartwatches, and Bluetooth headsets.

Yes, with reinforced backing and optimized thickness, IP68 is achievable for TPU membranes.

Yes, our TPU membranes come with adhesive backing (PSA) compatible with pick-and-place and ultrasonic welding processes.
