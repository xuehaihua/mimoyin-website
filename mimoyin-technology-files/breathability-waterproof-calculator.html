Frequently asked questions

What does "ml/min @ Ø10 mm orifice @ 7 kPa" mean?

It is the standard breathability test: total air flow (ml per minute) passing through a 10 mm diameter orifice under a 7 kPa pressure differential. Higher ml/min = more air-permeable = "breathable".

Can I convert breathability directly to an IP rating?

Indirectly. Breathability sets the waterproof ceiling (see trade-off table). Map the resulting water-column height to IP67 (1 m / 30 min) or IP68 (continuous immersion) per IEC 60529. The calculator gives the water-column estimate; IP grading also depends on enclosure sealing.

Why is ePTFE better than PU or non-fluorinated film?

ePTFE reaches 2–50 m waterproof with ≤1 dB sound loss and -40~+260 °C range, while PU / non-fluorinated films are limited to ~1–2 m waterproof and narrower temperature or acoustic windows.
